The short version

The first time a vendor ledger wouldn't match the bank statement, nobody around me could tell me why — it was a break that had been carried forward for three months before it landed on my desk. I traced it back line by line until the pattern showed up, then rebuilt the reconciliation process so it wouldn't happen again. That's more or less how I've approached the work ever since.

Four years now, deep in SAP FI/MM — reconciling bank, vendor, and GL accounts, running AP/AR end to end, and owning Procure-to-Pay across two organisations. Somewhere in there I also built the Power BI reporting suite my current team still runs every week, mostly because I got tired of chasing the same numbers across a dozen disconnected Excel files. I'm still working through the FMVA curriculum on my own time, reading into treasury operations and fund structures, building toward more analytical, senior-level finance work.
